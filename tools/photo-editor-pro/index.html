Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Photo Editor Pro really free?
Yes — completely free forever. No registration, no watermarks, no limits. Open, edit and export as many photos as you like.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. Every adjustment and effect is applied locally using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.
Where do the background photos come from?
Background photos are searched live from Pexels and Pixabay — millions of free, royalty-free stock images. You can also upload your own background from your device.
What formats can I export?
You can export as PNG (lossless), JPG (smaller files with quality control) or WebP (optimized for the web).
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The editor is fully responsive and optimized for touch on phones and tablets. Open from your gallery or files app.
